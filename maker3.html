🎥 Track lanes

M = mute, S = solo. Drums: tap steps on/off. Pitched tracks: tap a bar block to switch that bar off/on. The little ▶ previews one track. Add as many extra tracks as you like — they all play in sync.

🎤 Mixer

Faders set each track’s level, knobs set left/right pan. Changes apply to play and to the WAV export.

🕮 Sound library

Real archive sounds (JAH-SOUND records), generated deterministically — the same record, every visit.

🤖 AI host

AI choices (all editable above)

🚀 Starter templates

One tap loads a working song you can press Play on right away. Every template is built fresh by the Signature engine — same tap, same song, every time.

🥁 Beat maker

Tap squares on/off to build your own drum beat. Press Play to hear it loop. 16 steps = 1 bar; the darker left edges mark the 4 beats.

🎹 Playable instruments

Tap the keys and pads — everything plays live in your browser. Piano keys also work with your computer keyboard (A–K).

🎼 Note writer (piano roll)

Click a square to place a note. Drag a note left/right to move it, up/down to change its pitch. Click a note without moving to remove it. 16 steps = 1 bar.

🔁 Loop library original & royalty-free

Every loop here is an original Signature loop — synthesized fresh in your browser by the Signature engine. Free to use in your songs: no royalties, no samples from anywhere else.

💡 AI idea starter

Type a few words about the vibe you want. The studio helper turns them into a real song idea — same words, same idea, every time.

🎤 Sound effects (FX)

Plain-language effects for each track and the master. They shape the sound when you press Play and in your WAV/MP3 exports.

🎵 Audio → notes (audio-to-MIDI)

Turn a recording into a note list. Works best on single-note lines (one note at a time — humming, a flute line, a bass line). Chords and full mixes will confuse it — that’s normal for this kind of tool.

🎤 Voice tools

🔇 Vocal remover (center-channel)

Removes sound panned to the center of a stereo file (where lead vocals usually sit). Karaoke-style DSP — not AI stem separation.

Works best on studio stereo mixes. Mono files and hard-panned vocals won’t change much — that’s how center-channel removal works.

🗣 Voice effect (pitch + formant)

A voice effect that shifts pitch and voice character — chipmunk to deep giant. Just an effect, applied in your browser.

✨ Master my mix

One tap runs your mix through a mastering chain preset — warm EQ, gentle glue compressor, limiter, normalize. It’s a fixed recipe, not magic: it polishes a good mix, it can’t fix a bad one.
