🎤 Vocal Studio

Enter lyrics, pick a voice — a created voice or your own voice. The vocal synth sings it. Plainly: these are synthesized voices, not human singers.

Created voices (Nova, Ember, Drift, Stone) are formant-synthesis voices built on this page. "Your own voice" records or uploads your sample and pitch-maps it onto the melody — a resynthesis of your recording.

🔒 Your voice, your device. Recording and uploads are accepted as wav, mp3, webm or ogg (whatever your browser can decode), up to 10 MB. Your sample is decoded in this browser's memory only — it is never uploaded anywhere and never leaves your device. It is replaced when you record or upload a new sample, and it is gone when you reload or close the page.

🎶 Backup Singers

✨ Vocal Cleanup

Upload your vocals — the AI cleans them up: de-rumble, de-hiss, compression, leveling. Re-sing clean detects your melody and has the vocal synth sing it back alike, cleaned up — the re-sung voice is synthesized, not a human singer.

Honest limits: cleanup cannot fix hard clipping or heavy distortion. Record with peaks near −12 dB for best results.

💿 Make a CD

Pick songs from the archive — the site renders each to CD-quality audio, builds a cue sheet, and packages a disc image download with step-by-step burning instructions.

Browsers cannot drive a CD burner directly, so this page prepares the disc image; you burn it with your own burner software using the included guide. First: does your system have a CD burner?
