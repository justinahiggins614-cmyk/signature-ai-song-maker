🎤 Or sing it yourself

🔒 Your voice, your device. Recording and uploads are accepted as wav, mp3, webm or ogg (whatever your browser can decode), up to 10 MB. Your sample is decoded in this browser's memory only — it is never uploaded anywhere and never leaves your device. It is replaced when you record or upload a new sample, and it is gone when you reload or close the page.

💿 Burn a CD

Your finished song can go on a real CD. Browsers can't drive a disc burner directly, so this builds a disc image (your song + a cue sheet) for your own burner software.

⚠️ Browsers can't burn a disc directly. Take the disc image to your computer's CD burner program (like Windows Media Player or iTunes) to burn a real disc. The .zip holds your song's .wav plus this cue sheet.
